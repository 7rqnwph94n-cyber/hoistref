# YALE — Service Intervals

Per Yale CPV/CPVF Operating Instructions (CMCO 2018).

---

## Daily — operator (pre-shift)

| Item | Check |
|---|---|
| Pendant control switch + cables | Visual; no cuts, abrasion, exposed conductors |
| Brake function | Including E-stop button |
| Limit switch function | Both upper and lower |
| Overload circuit breaker (slip clutch) function | Trip behaviour during a controlled lift |
| Trolley (if fitted) | Runway free, end stops in place |
| **If ambient < 0 °C** | Lift and lower small load 2-3 times to free brake |

---

## Before starting work — competent operator

| Item | Check |
|---|---|
| Hoist + trolley + chains + load-bearing components | Visual for defects |
| Brake | Short cycle of lift/lower in both directions |
| Chain lubrication | Visual; lubricate every month OR every 50 operating hours |
| Chain end stop on idle (free) strand | In place |
| Chain reeving (multi-fall) | No twists |
| Hooks (load + suspension) | No deformation, cracks, damage, abrasion, corrosion |
| Limit switch | Lifting against housing must stop immediately |
| Trolley traverse | No defects |
| Trolley wheel clearance | 2 mm each side / 4 mm total max |

---

## Every 3 months OR 200 operating hours — chain measurement

Chain inspection at the **earlier** of the two intervals — sooner than most brands' annual-only schedules.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Chain — single link reduction | Replace if d < 0.9 × d_nominal (10% rule) |
| Chain — single pitch | Replace if elongated > 5% |
| Chain — 11-pitch | Replace if elongated > **2%** (Yale-tight rule) |
| Chain visual | No cracks, deformation, bends, corrosion |

Use point/needle calliper for accurate worn-point measurement.

---

## Every month OR 50 operating hours — chain lubrication

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Clean | Vapour degrease or alkaline dip — NOT acid/pickling (hydrogen embrittlement) |
| Tension-free | Lubricate with chain slack so oil penetrates joints |
| Apply | Yale-approved chain lubricant |
| **Burning off old lube** | NOT permitted |

Yale claims **20-30× chain life extension** with careful lubrication.

---

## Annual — competent person inspection

All daily + before-starting items, plus:

### Chain & hooks (detailed)

- Full chain wear measurement (record actual values)
- Hook wear measurement (load + suspension)
- Hook safety catch + bearing inspection

### Brake

- Air gap measurement (feeler gauge) — replace if at max permissible
- Brake removal procedure if approaching limit (SOP 8)

### Slip clutch

- **Full test and adjustment** — recorded in test log
- 60-second max op time + 20-min cool-down rule applied
- Use force-limit factor formula to verify suspension structure

### Threaded connections

- Inspect ALL nuts, screws, locking devices
- Pre-coated single-use safety screws (2-strand): **never reused**

### Chain container

- Condition + capacity vs lift height

### Suspension bolt

- Connection between hoist and suspension bracket / trolley
- Out-of-service inspection

### Trolley (if fitted)

- Side plates, wheels, crossbars, fasteners
- Wheel wear ≤ 5% diameter reduction
- Flange clearance: 2 mm each side / 4 mm total max
- Uneven flange wear → trolley canting → investigate runway

### Service-life calculation

- Read theoretical service life: 1Am/M4 = 800 h ≈ 10 years normal use
- Calculate remaining safe working period
- Trigger GO at theoretical life expiry

### Documentation

- LOLER Form of Report
- **Slip clutch result MUST be recorded in test log** — examiners expect this
- Yale's "competent person" definition aligns with LOLER Reg 10

---

## After 10 years OR end of theoretical service life — General Overhaul

Per FEM 9.755. Yale's standard 1Am/M4 classification = 800 h theoretical = ~ 10 years normal use.

If hoist is older than 10 years AND no GO evidence → LOLER finding. Inspector will expect:
- Annual records
- Brake replacement records (if reached air gap max)
- Chain replacement records (if reached wear limit)
- Slip clutch adjustment log entries
- GO entries if applicable

---

## Conditions that **shorten** intervals

- Multi-shift / continuous duty
- Dust ingress / abrasive environment
- Outdoor / corrosive
- High ambient temperature
- Below 0 °C operation (cold-start procedure required)
- High overload-event frequency
- Operating outside FEM duty classification

---

## Below 0 °C operation

- **Cold-start procedure:** lift and lower small load 2-3 times before use
- Verify lubricant grade suitable for low-temperature operation
- More frequent chain inspection (cold makes embrittlement issues worse)

---

## VFD-controlled motorised trolley (CPE-VFM / E-VTE)

If fitted, additional inspection items:

- VFD parameters: confirm unchanged from factory (Yale-authorised)
- VFD cooling air path: clean, unobstructed
- Insulation test: disconnect VFD before megger
- Thermal cool wait after operation before maintenance
