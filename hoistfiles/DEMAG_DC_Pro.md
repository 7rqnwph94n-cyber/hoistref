# DEMAG DC-Pro / DCM-Pro Reference Notes

**Source:** Demag Cranes & Components operating instructions / component parts (IS 817, publication 900 426 49 720, 11/2005 issue).

Applies to: **DC-Pro 1, DC-Pro 2, DC-Pro 5, DC-Pro 10, DC-Pro 20** and **DCM-Pro 1, DCM-Pro 2, DCM-Pro 5 (Manulift variants)**.

**Scope filter for our app (up to 2 tonnes):** DC-Pro 1, DC-Pro 2, DC-Pro 5, DC-Pro 10. DC-Pro 20 goes to 2,000 kg on 2/1 reeving so is borderline — include it.

Note: a newer **DC-Com** manual exists and is largely the same architecture. Values below are from the classic DC-Pro manual; cross-check when a DC-Com unit is on site.

## Chain wear limits (DEMAG spec, per DIN 685 part 5)

Two measurement methods permitted: chain gauge (part no. 836 025 44) or calliper.

Calliper method: measure 11 links as 2×3 links + 1×5 links; sum total must not exceed the limit 'a'.

| Model | Chain d×t | a (11 links max, mm) | t (single link max, mm) | d_m (min link dia, mm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DC-Pro 1 | 4.2×12.2 | 144.7 | 12.8 | 3.8 |
| DC-Pro 2 | 4.2×12.2 | 144.7 | 12.8 | 3.8 |
| DC-Pro 5 | 5.2×15.3 | 180.3 | 15.9 | 4.8 |
| DC-Pro 10 | 7.2×21.4 | 253.0 | 22.4 | 6.7 |
| DC-Pro 20 | 7.2×21.4 | 253.0 | 22.4 | 6.7 |

Minimum link diameter rule: **d_m = 0.9 × d**, measured across the worn contact faces: d_m = (d1 + d2) / 2.

Chain replacement: **always replace the entire chain set** — new chain alone is not supported, the set includes chain + sprocket + chain guide + plates + buffers + anchorage (for 2/1).

**Lubrication:** Demag specifies their gear grease part 665 009 44. Before commissioning, before the first test lift, and during operation at service intervals. Dry film lubricant required in abrasive environments (emery, sand). Stamped "DEMAG" on every 12th link confirms genuine chain.

**DEMAG-specific point:** if new chain doesn't run smoothly over the sprocket, call after-sales. Non-Demag chain voids the EC conformity and liability.

## Brake

**Type:** Electrically released spring-applied disc brake, arranged on the load side (ahead of slip clutch in the load path). Automatic application on power cut. Required no maintenance per Demag — brake wear check every **10 years** (DC-Pro 1/2/5) or **5 years** (DC-Pro 10/20).

**Wear check procedure:**
1. Disconnect power supply.
2. Open electrical equipment cover.
3. Loosen brake connector.
4. Dismantle brake — three M5 screws (tightening torque **5.5 Nm**).
5. Measure distance from brake rear to brake disc with depth/calliper gauge:
   - First measurement: non-actuated (relaxed) state
   - Second measurement: actuated state — press brake disc against springs (G-clamp can be used)
6. Difference between the two readings = brake wear.
7. **If difference > 0.45 mm, replace the brake.** Otherwise brake is serviceable.

**Reassembly:** lightly grease the V-ring area on the brake base, ensure continuous sealing lip contacts the brake rear side fully.

**Functional test:** slip clutch is the emergency backup. If braking distance is unusually long, have an experienced technician inspect without delay.

## Slip clutch

**Architecture:** arranged between motor shaft and pinion shaft. Functions as:
- Overload protection
- Emergency limit stop device for highest and lowest hook position
- EC directive-compliant load control device for capacity ≥ 1,000 kg

**The brake is arranged AHEAD of the slip clutch in the load path** — so if the clutch trips or slips, the brake still holds the load. This is a key Demag safety feature.

**Monitoring:** the control electronics monitor pulse wheel feedback; slip detection causes automatic cut-out. Warning displayed as "SLIP LIFTING V1/V2" or "SLIP LOWERING V1/V2" on the 7-segment display.

**Factory setting:** do not adjust under normal operating conditions — the clutch is set at the factory. Increase of tripping torque beyond factory setting is **not permitted**. Adjustment by authorised specialists only.

**Annual check:** recommended with friction force checking device, part no. 836 708 44.

**Mandatory annual check** for capacity > 1,000 kg (DC-Pro 10 and DC-Pro 20).

**Function test method:** without load, fit the chain limit stop above the hook fittings (remove it from the free chain end first) and run at creep speed against the guide plate — limit switches must NOT be actuated. Indicators of correct function:
- Hoist motor fan still turning with no lifting motion
- Display shows "SLIP LIFTING V1"

Relocate limit stop back to the unloaded chain end after testing.

---

## Limit switches

Two independent systems:

1. **Electrical limit switches** (upper and lower) — operational limits, stop motion at intended endpoints. These prevent slip clutch being used as an end-stop in normal operation.

2. **Emergency limit stop device** — the chain limit stops on the chain ends engage the slip clutch, which then acts as an ultimate cut-out via the control electronics.

**When upper or lower limit trips:** only opposite motion available. Chain limit stop trip → "SLIP LIFTING/LOWERING" error → motor cut, brake applied.

## Electrical / Control

**Control voltage:** 24 V contactor control as standard.
**Control pendants:** DSC (2-axis lift/lower), DSE-C (3-axis with trolley), DSM-C (Manulift variant with spade handle).
**IP rating:** 
- Hoist body: IP55
- Pendant: IP65

**E-stop:** on all pendants. Press latches, twist to release. When actuated: motor disconnected from supply immediately, mechanical brake applied. Off-position constraint means hoist won't resume until buttons released.

**Display codes** (7-segment display, lower side of electrical cover):
- `3-second pause then number` = operating hours
- `LIFTING V1/V2`, `LOWERING V1/V2` = normal states
- `SLIP LIFTING/LOWERING` = slip clutch active (warning)
- `E1` to `E7` = error codes (see troubleshooting table)

**Error codes summary (DC-Pro safety failure states — operation only after repair):**
| Code | Cause |
|---|---|
| E1 | Chain hoist blocked OR hardware error |
| E2 | Drive blocked OR speed measurement failure |
| E3 | Direction cannot be switched |
| E4 | Load > 160% being lifted — slipping clutch issue |
| E5 | Upper limit switch fault |
| E6 | Lower limit switch fault |

## Operating conditions

- **Ambient temperature:** −10°C to +40°C operation
- **Transport/storage:** −25°C to +55°C
- **Humidity:** max 80% RH
- **Altitude:** up to 1,000 m
- **IP rating:** IP55

Outdoor operation requires weather protection. Outside these ranges — consult Demag.

## General overhaul (GO)

Theoretical service life depends on FEM classification. Actual service life determined annually during inspection.

**Trigger:** at **90% of theoretical service life** (typically 8-10 years under normal correctly-classified use) or latest at end of theoretical service life.

**Parts replaced at GO (in addition to annual inspection work):**
- Gearbox housing with joined gearing parts
- Gear oil + gearbox cover with seal
- Connecting elements
- Shaft sealing rings, bearings, plugs
- Brake

GO must be carried out by manufacturer or authorised specialist. Entry in test and inspection booklet required for continued operation.

## Inspection & maintenance schedule (key items)

### Before putting into operation (first time)
- Capacity plate voltage/frequency matches supply
- Supporting structure adequate
- Safety devices fitted and effective
- Clearances and safety distances OK
- E-stop function verified

### When starting work (daily)
- E-stop test
- Direction of movement
- 7-segment display
- Chain lubrication (check — more frequent under heavy-duty)
- Lifting/lowering limit switch operation
- Pendant cable and casing inspection
- Brake operation
- Hook + safety catch

### Annual
- Read operating hours (remaining service life)
- Electrical switchgear + wiring
- Slip clutch function
- Suspension, suspension bracket, securing elements (retaining clips)
- Securing screws on hook assembly
- Hooks for cracks, deformation, wear (measure against `f` limit)
- Hook safety catch deformation
- Hook bearing wear
- Chain sprocket + chain guide
- Chain fastening + chain collector box fastening
- **Chain wear measurement** (11-link method, against table values)
- Chain for deformation / damage / cracks / pitting / elongation
- Securing elements for tight fit + corrosion
- Corrosion protection top-up
- Electrical enclosure + gearbox for leakages
- Trolley, load bar, buffer condition

### Every 10 years (DC-Pro 1/2/5) or 5 years (DC-Pro 10/20)
- Brake wear check (procedure above)

## What's different vs STAHL ST

Worth noting so field workflow adapts:

1. **Chain classification** — DEMAG cites DIN 685-5 for the inspection procedure AND EN 818-7 quality class T for the chain itself (STAHL cites DIN 685-5 only).
2. **Brake position in load path** — DEMAG brake is ahead of slip clutch, so slip = still held. STAHL's slip clutch is a combined brake/clutch unit on ST05 and is an emergency backup on ST10+.
3. **Brake wear check interval** — DEMAG every 5 or 10 years by model (not annual). STAHL is annual.
4. **Clutch factory setting** — DEMAG says "do not adjust", factory set, annual check only. STAHL permits adjustment within 110-160% of rated load per EN 14492-2.
5. **Error codes** — DEMAG has a 7-segment display with E1-E7 fault codes. STAHL does not.
6. **General overhaul at 90% of theoretical service life** — DEMAG makes this explicit. STAHL refers to FEM 9.755 but doesn't give the 90% rule directly.
7. **Chain replacement philosophy** — DEMAG chain MUST be replaced as a complete chain set (chain + sprocket + guide + plates). STAHL permits chain-only replacement with orientation rules.
8. **Configuration** — DEMAG doesn't use a letter variant code like STAHL's ST05/10/20/30/32/50/60. Uses DC-Pro 1/2/5/10/20 where the number indicates capacity class, with V1 (single speed) or V2 (two speed) suffix for motor variant.

## Typical fault → field action

| Symptom | First check |
|---|---|
| Display dark, no motion | Mains supply, fuse, connection cable, service enclosure connector |
| E-stop pressed latch | Twist to release, check trolley dummy plug if fitted, check pendant cable connections |
| Direction wrong | Two phases of mains connection swapped — isolate, swap L2 and L3 |
| No lifting but will lower | Mains phase missing / motor blocked / slip clutch at 160% — check fuse, motor, load |
| Fast lifting cuts out under load | Overloaded — reduce load |
| Slow lowering cuts out | Lowering speed too high or supply issue |
| Chain blocked on lift | Run-in check, possibly replace chain set |
