# GIS GP Reference Notes

**Manufacturer:** GIS AG — Swiss Lifting Solutions. Luzernerstrasse 50, CH-6247 Schötz, Switzerland. 60+ years manufacturing in Switzerland.

**Source:** GIS GP Translation of the Original Instruction Manual (9500.9002.1, 02.19 issue), covers GP 250, GP 500, GP 1000 variants with single-phase and three-phase options.

**Scope filter (up to 2 tonnes):** GP 250 (125-500 kg), GP 500 (250-1000 kg), GP 1000 (500-2000 kg), GPM 250 (miniature version). All variants within scope.

**Important:** GIS also produces the **GCH** series (newer, 2005+) and the older **EM/EME** series (1992-2005 discontinued). This document is GP only — service approach varies between series. Check nameplate carefully.

---

## Model range and coding (reading a GIS nameplate)

GIS uses a structured nomenclature: **GP [size]/[falls][duty-code]**

Size numbers: 250 / 500 / 1000 (approximate capacity class — actual capacity depends on duty classification and falls).

Falls: `1` or `2` (single or double chain).

**Duty codes:**
- `NF` = standard 2-speed (main/fine), 8/2-pole motor
- `SF` = 2-speed high-speed variant, 16/4-pole motor
- `NL` = single-speed low-speed variant (4-pole)
- `N` = single-speed standard (2-pole)

**Phase designator:** `1Ph` added if single-phase (230V).

## Chain specification — GIS Profile Steel Chain

**Key differentiator:** GIS uses **profile steel chain** (also called 8SS chain), NOT conventional round-section. The chain is case-hardened and manganese phosphated. The profile provides about **15% more capacity at identical nominal diameter** vs round steel chain. It's rated **grade T / DAT 800 N/mm² per DIN EN 818-7**.

**Safety factor:** at least **8× at FEM 2m / ISO M5** — higher than some round-chain competitors.

### Chain dimensions and wear limits (from GIS Table 3-4)

Measurements taken per DIN 685 part 5 / DIN EN 818-7.

| Model | Nominal d × t | a max (11 links, mm) | t max (1 link, mm) | d_min (0.9 × d, mm) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GP 250 | 3.75 × 10.75 | **120.6** | **11.3** | **3.4** |
| GP 500 | 5.25 × 15 | **168.3** | **15.7** | **4.7** |
| GP 1000 | 7.45 × 23 | **258.1** | **24.1** | **6.7** |

**Replace chain if ANY of:**
- 11-link measurement **exceeds a_max**
- Single-link measurement **exceeds t_max**
- Link diameter (mean of d1 and d2 at worn point) **falls below d_min** (0.9 × nominal)

### Chain installation rules
- **Welded seam of chain links must face INWARD on the chain wheel** (toward hoist body)
- **Only original GIS chains** — welded joints on chains NOT permitted
- On 2-fall: **no longitudinal twisting** of chain allowed
- **Safety suspension measurement k1** (1-fall): GP 250/500 = 41 mm, GP 1000 = 43 mm
- **Safety suspension measurement k2** (2-fall): GP 250/500 = 52 mm, GP 1000 = 62 mm

### Chain lubrication
- Use **GIS chain oil** or **SAE 15W-40** (creeping gear oil)
- Must be present on internal, contacting and rubbing surfaces of chain links at ALL times
- Before start-up AND during operation, chain must be oiled along its full length
- Lubrication by submersion or oil can

## Brake — DC spring-loaded single-disc

**Architecture:** DC-operated, spring-loaded single-disc brake with two friction surfaces. Springs apply braking torque; solenoid releases. Operates on DC current. **Must hold nominal load in power-free mode.**

**The slip clutch is installed BEFORE the brake system in the load path.** This means:
- Clutch protects hoist from overload
- If clutch trips, brake still holds the load
- Clutch also acts as emergency stop at highest/lowest hook positions

**No air gap adjustment is possible.** If air gap reaches max value, coating and brake disc both need replacement.

### Brake specifications (GIS Table 3-3)

| Model | Nominal air gap `a` | Max air gap `a_max` | Holding torque | Screw tightening torque |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GP 250 | 0.3 mm (+0.1 / −0.05) | **0.7 mm** | 3 Nm | 3 Nm |
| GP 500 | 0.3 mm (+0.1 / −0.05) | **0.7 mm** | 6 Nm | 3 Nm |
| GP 1000 | 0.3 mm (+0.15 / −0.05) | **0.9 mm** | 12 Nm | 6 Nm |

**Brake coil voltage must equal the operating voltage.**

**Field measurement:** measure air gap with feeler gauge. If reading is at or above a_max, replace the coating AND disc.

## Slip clutch

**Factory setting: 125% of rated load.** Power-limiting factor φDAL = 1.6 per DIN EN 14492-2. For the lowest two load capacities in the tables, the factor is ≤ 2.

**Key points:**
- Reliably prevents overloading
- Installed **before the brake** in the load path (failure-safe design)
- Adjustment and testing **ONLY by authorised personnel**
- **Must be recorded in the inspection pass**
- If nominal load won't lift or lifting speed is delayed, the clutch needs adjustment
- Coating is wear-resistant — not user-serviceable

**Forces from clutch adjustment must be absorbable by suspension parts.**

|---|---|---|---|
| 1:1 | Black | 20 m | 10 m |
| 1:1.5 | Red | 30 m | 15 m |
| 1:3 | Yellow | 60 m | 30 m |
| 1:6 | Blue | 126 m | 63 m |

### Lift capacity by transmission (GP 1000)

| Transmission | Colour code | 1-fall lift | 2-fall lift |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1:1 | Black | 30 m | 15 m |
| 1:1.5 | Red | 45 m | 23 m |
| 1:3 | Yellow | 90 m | 45 m |
| 1:6 | Blue | 192 m | 96 m |

### Setting the limit switch
1. Before chain install / chain change, rocker switch (1) must be pressed to disable limit switch mechanically
2. After chain install: bring hook to highest position, turn red control wheel (rear) against upper cam
3. Enable rocker (must engage with control wheel)
4. Bring hook to lowest position, turn green control wheel (front) against lower cam
5. Enable rocker
6. **Check limit function** — limit stop and hook fittings must NOT contact housing

**Optional:** emergency-stop contacts with positive separation can be retrofitted downstream.

## Operating conditions

- **Ambient temperature:** **−15°C to +50°C** (wider than Kito's −20/+40, similar to Yale's −20/+50)
- **Humidity:** max 80% relative
- **Protection class:** **IP 65** (notably higher than competitors — typically IP55 for hoist body)
- **Electromagnetic compatibility:** immunity for industrial area
- **Control voltage:** 42 V (low voltage control)
- **Outdoor use:** weather protection recommended
- **Explosive environments:** not rated, do not use

## Service life and general overhaul

**Theoretical operating time by FEM classification:**

| Class | M3 / 1Bm | M4 / 1Am | M5 / 2m | M6 / 3m | M7 / 4m | M8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Full-load hours | 400 h | 800 h | 1600 h | 3200 h | 6300 h | 12500 h |

**Annual operating time calculation** (from GIS Table 4-1) is based on:
- Daily operating hours (from operator log or hour meter)
- Load collective `k` (from Table 1-1: light k<0.50, medium 0.50-0.63, heavy 0.63-0.80, very heavy 0.80-1.00)

**Example:** M4 hoist used with heavy load collective (k=0.80), 0.5-1h/day → 96 full-load hours per year → 800h theoretical / 96 = **8.3 years to general overhaul**.

### General overhaul (GO)
**Trigger:** theoretical operating time reached, OR no later than **10 years from commissioning** (whichever comes first) if no operating-data recording.

**Components checked/replaced at GO (from Table 4-2):**
- **Check for wear:** brake, motor shaft, gear teeth, antifriction bearings, sealings, chain wheel/chain guidance, suspension, load hook, trolley/load wheel, contactor, limit switch
- **Replace regardless:** chain (at latest at GO)

GO must be done by the manufacturer or an authorised specialist company. Inspector determines:
- New theoretical operating time allowed
- Maximum period until next GO

**Record in inspection pass.**

## Emergency stop

- Press red mushroom button → all three mains phases disconnected immediately → hoist stops
- Unlock by turning in indicated direction
- After E-stop: operator can only resume after an **authorised person** has verified that the cause is eliminated AND no danger remains

## Inspection pass (required document)

GIS **mandates an inspection pass** — a document created at commissioning and maintained throughout service life. Contents:
- Technical data
- Start-up date
- All servicing and maintenance entries
- Confirmed by competent person

**Incorrect or missing entries void the manufacturer's warranty.**

This is an excellent fit for LOLER documentation — the inspection pass IS essentially the LOLER Form of Report in GIS format.

## GIS-specific field gotchas

1. **Profile steel chain, not round** — chain replacements MUST be GIS profile chain, not generic round-section. Gauge/wear criteria are different.
2. **Chain welded seams face INWARD** (toward hoist body) over the sprocket — opposite to STAHL's outward rule. Easy to get wrong on a chain swap.
3. **Brake has NO air gap adjustment** — if at a_max, both coating AND disc get replaced as a set.
4. **Slip clutch is BEFORE the brake in the load path** — similar to DEMAG. Failure of clutch still leaves load held.
5. **Geared limit switch with transmission selection** — colour-coded (black/red/yellow/blue). If lift height changes (e.g. mezzanine removed), transmission must be swapped too.
6. **Inspection pass is mandatory** — incomplete entries void warranty, affect LOLER credibility.
7. **Operating temperature −15°C to +50°C** — wider than most. Useful for cold stores and some outdoor use.
8. **IP 65 rating** — higher than most chain hoists. Makes GIS a common choice for washdown, food, and exposed outdoor applications.
9. **Inching operations cause malfunctions on single-phase models** — explicit warning in manual.
10. **Four different limit switch transmissions** — always check the colour code matches the actual lift height before commissioning.

## Differences vs STAHL / DEMAG / Yale / Kito

1. **Profile steel chain is unique to GIS** (and LP series, same manufacturer). Other four brands all use round-section chain.
2. **No air gap adjustment on brake** — all four other brands allow adjustment within a range.
3. **Slip clutch at 125% factory** — same as STAHL; lower than Yale's 145%, different from DEMAG's factory-set-don't-touch.
4. **Geared limit switch** — most precise of the five brands; can function as routine operational limit.
5. **Suspension wear limits by part** (load hook h, suspension h, eyebolt h, opening l) — most structured wear spec of any of the brands.
6. **IP 65 standard** vs IP 55 for others.
7. **Inspection pass as formal requirement** — most explicit of any brand about maintaining a service log.
8. **Manufacturer scope** — GIS is relatively small (vs CMCO/Demag/Kito) but Swiss-made with strong quality reputation. Service parts via official distributors.

## Quick field workflow (GIS GP)

1. **Data plate:** type, serial, year, capacity, FEM/ISO classification
2. **Inspection pass:** present? Up to date? Commissioning data recorded?
3. **Profile chain:** check all three wear criteria with point caliper (11-link, 1-link, link diameter)
4. **Welded seams orientation:** inward over sprocket (check a sample)
5. **Limit switch transmission:** colour correct for actual lift height?
6. **Limit switch function:** upper AND lower stop before housing contact
7. **Brake air gap:** feeler gauge, compare to nominal and max values per model
8. **Clutch function:** lifts rated load without slip, doesn't slip below rated
9. **Hook wear:** measure h and l, compare to table
10. **Suspension wear:** measure h on used suspension style (hook or eyebolt)
11. **Torque sample:** random fasteners at suspension, chain end, hook block
12. **Sealings, cables, strain relief:** visual
